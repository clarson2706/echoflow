# EchoFlow product context

register: product

## Users
People who think out loud: students and professionals who dictate notes, messages to AI assistants and emails, and sit in meetings or lectures. They want their speech to come out as what they meant to say, and a place to find it later.

## Purpose
Hold a key, speak, get clean text in any app. A note mode that listens and ends with a finished note. A dashboard that remembers everything locally.

## Brand and tone
Quiet, composed, old money: ivory and cream, deep forest green, muted brass, a serif voice for headings. Liquid glass surfaces used for floating layers only (rail, detail panel, live bar, overlay), never as decoration on every block. Calm copy, no exclamation marks, no em dashes.

## Anti-references
Neon AI tools, dark terminal dashboards, gradient text, identical card grids, hero metrics, emoji-heavy chrome.

## Principles
1. The text is the hero; chrome recedes.
2. Local and private, said plainly.
3. Familiar app structure (rail, list, detail) so it disappears into the task.
